## Design Context

### Users
Wes reviews overnight research in the morning. He needs to understand verified progress, identify wasted effort, pause or continue experiments, tune limits, and review release evidence without a terminal.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, candid. Evidence earns confidence; small numerical changes are not celebrated as societal impact.

### Aesthetic Direction
A light editorial research notebook: warm paper, dark ink, restrained green and rust for distinct statuses, clear tables and readable typography. No decorative charts or model hype. This implements the approved concise morning-review surface.

### Design Principles
1. Explain what ran and what was actually verified before presenting totals.
2. Distinguish legacy observations, confirmed benchmark gains and generalization evidence.
3. Put human judgments in accessible controls with clear outcomes.
4. Show incomplete and zero-work runs prominently.
5. Keep technical details available beneath concise summaries.
